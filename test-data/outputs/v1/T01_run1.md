### 📅 The 7-Day Study Timetable

#### **DAY 1: Logic, Core Concepts & Data Modeling**
*   **Discrete Maths:** Mathematical Logic, Truth Tables, Propositional & Predicate Logic.
*   **Operating Systems:** Introduction to OS, Process vs. Thread, Process State Diagrams.
*   **DBMS:** ER Diagrams, ER-to-Relational Mapping, Keys (Primary, Foreign, Candidate).

#### **DAY 2: Proofs, Scheduling & Relational Algebra**
*   **Discrete Maths:** Methods of Proof (Direct, Contradiction) & Mathematical Induction.
*   **Operating Systems:** CPU Scheduling Algorithms (FCFS, SJF, Priority, Round Robin) + numerical practice.
*   **DBMS:** Relational Algebra (Select, Project, Joins, Set operations).

#### **DAY 3: Sets, Synchronization & SQL**
*   **Discrete Maths:** Set Theory, Relations (Equivalence, Partial Order), Functions.
*   **Operating Systems:** Process Synchronization, Critical Section Problem, Semaphores, Classic Sync Problems (Producer-Consumer, Dining Philosophers).
*   **DBMS:** SQL Basics to Intermediate (DDL/DML, GROUP BY, HAVING, Joins, Subqueries).

#### **DAY 4: Counting, Deadlocks & Normalization (Crucial Day!)**
*   **Discrete Maths:** Combinatorics, Permutations & Combinations, Pigeonhole Principle.
*   **Operating Systems:** Deadlocks (Conditions, Prevention, Avoidance - Banker’s Algorithm).
*   **DBMS:** Functional Dependencies & Normalization (1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF) + solve normalization problems.

#### **DAY 5: Graphs, Memory Management & Transactions**
*   **Discrete Maths:** Graph Theory Basics, Euler & Hamiltonian Paths, Trees, Graph Coloring.
*   **Operating Systems:** Memory Management, Paging, Segmentation, Virtual Memory, Page Replacement Algos (FIFO, LRU, Optimal).
*   **DBMS:** Transactions & ACID Properties, Schedules & Serializability (Conflict & View).

#### **DAY 6: Advanced Topics & Full PYQ Practice**
*   **Discrete Maths:** Recurrence Relations & Generating Functions.
*   **Operating Systems:** Disk Scheduling Algorithms (FCFS, SCAN, C-SCAN) & File Systems.
*   **DBMS:** Concurrency Control (Lock-based, 2PL) & Indexing (B-Trees / B+ Trees basics).
*   *Evening:* Solve at least **1 past paper question** for each subject under timed conditions.

#### **DAY 7: Mock Exams & Weak Spot Patching**
*   **Morning:** Solve Previous Year Question Papers (PYQs) for Discrete Maths.
*   **Afternoon:** Solve PYQs for Operating Systems.
*   **Evening:** Solve PYQs for DBMS.
*   **Night:** Review formulas, truth tables, and algorithm steps. Sleep well!

### 💡 Subject-Specific Exam Strategy Tips

1.  **Discrete Mathematics:**
    *   *Don't just read—write!* You cannot revise math passively. Solve at least 5-10 numericals per topic.
    *   Focus heavily on **Induction, Graph Theory, and Recurrence Relations** as these usually carry high marks.

2.  **Operating Systems:**
    *   Master the **numericals**: CPU Scheduling, Page Replacement, Banker's Algorithm, and Disk Scheduling are direct scoring areas.
    *   Draw diagrams (e.g., Process State Transition) in exams to get full marks for theory questions.

3.  **DBMS:**
    *   Practice writing actual **SQL queries** and **Normalization decomposition steps**.
    *   Understand *how* to find Candidate Keys quickly—it's the foundation for solving Normalization questions.
