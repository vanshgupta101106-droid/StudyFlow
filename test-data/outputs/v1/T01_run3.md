Since your exams are approaching, the best approach is an **interleaved study schedule** (studying multiple subjects a day rather than one whole subject all day). This improves memory retention and prevents burnout.

Here is a comprehensive **7-Day Intensive Study Plan** designed around a **6 to 8-hour daily study routine**.

If you have a full day to study, use this breakdown. (If you have classes/work, compress these blocks into your available time).

*   **Late Morning Block (11:00 AM – 1:00 PM):** Problem Solving & Mathematics

### 📅 7-Day Exam-Ready Timetable

#### **DAY 1: Fundamentals & Core Concepts**
*   **Morning (OS):** Introduction to OS, System Calls, Process State Transitions, Threads.
*   **Mid-Day (Discrete Maths):** Set Theory, Relations, Functions, Equivalence Relations.
*   **Afternoon (DBMS):** ER Diagrams, Relational Model Concepts, Keys (Primary, Candidate, Foreign).
*   **Evening Revision:** Practice 5 ER Diagram problems + 10 Discrete Maths set theory questions.

#### **DAY 2: Scheduling, Logic & Normalization (High Weightage)**
*   **Morning (OS):** CPU Scheduling Algorithms (FCFS, SJF, Priority, Round Robin) + **Solve Numericals**.
*   **Mid-Day (Discrete Maths):** Propositional & Predicate Logic, Truth Tables, Logical Equivalences.
*   **Afternoon (DBMS):** Functional Dependencies & **Normalization (1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF)**.
*   **Evening Revision:** Solve 3 Normalization numericals + 3 CPU Scheduling numericals.

#### **DAY 3: Synchronization, Combinatorics & SQL**
*   **Morning (OS):** Process Synchronization, Critical Section Problem, Semaphores, Classic IPC Problems (Producer-Consumer, Dining Philosophers).
*   **Mid-Day (Discrete Maths):** Combinatorics, Permutations & Combinations, Pigeonhole Principle.
*   **Afternoon (DBMS):** SQL Queries (Joins, Group By, Having, Nested Subqueries).
*   **Evening Revision:** Write out 10 complex SQL queries by hand; review Semaphore pseudo-code.

#### **DAY 4: Deadlocks, Proofs & Transactions**
*   **Morning (OS):** Deadlocks (Conditions, Prevention, Avoidance - Banker's Algorithm, Detection).
*   **Mid-Day (Discrete Maths):** Mathematical Induction, Recurrence Relations.
*   **Afternoon (DBMS):** Transaction Processing, ACID Properties, Schedules & Serializability (Conflict & View).
*   **Evening Revision:** Solve 2 Banker’s Algorithm problems + 3 Recurrence Relation problems.

#### **DAY 5: Memory Management, Graphs & Concurrency**
*   **Morning (OS):** Memory Management (Paging, Segmentation, Page Replacement Algorithms: FIFO, LRU, Optimal).
*   **Mid-Day (Discrete Maths):** Graph Theory (Trees, Eulerian & Hamiltonian paths, Planar Graphs, Graph Coloring).
*   **Afternoon (DBMS):** Concurrency Control (Lock-based protocols, 2PL, Timestamp Ordering), Indexing (B-Trees, B+ Trees basics).
*   **Evening Revision:** Solve Page Fault numericals + Graph theory proofs.

#### **DAY 6: Storage, Group Theory & Advanced Topics**
*   **Morning (OS):** Virtual Memory, Thrashing, Disk Scheduling Algorithms (FCFS, SSTF, SCAN, C-SCAN).
*   **Mid-Day (Discrete Maths):** Algebraic Structures (Groups, Monoids, Rings, Lattices/Hasse Diagrams).
*   **Afternoon (DBMS):** File Organization, Hashing, Relational Algebra (Select, Project, Joins, Division).
*   **Evening Revision:** Solve Disk Scheduling numericals + Relational Algebra expressions.

#### **DAY 7: Full Mock Tests & Weak Spot Patching**
*   **Morning:** Solve Previous Year Question (PYQ) Paper – **Operating Systems** (2 Hours).
*   **Mid-Day:** Solve PYQ Paper – **Discrete Mathematics** (2 Hours).
*   **Afternoon:** Solve PYQ Paper – **DBMS** (2 Hours).
*   **Evening:** Analyze errors, review formula sheets, and rest.

### 💡 Subject-Specific Tips for Maximum Marks

1.  **Discrete Mathematics (DM):**
    *   *Do not just read solutions.* DM requires **pen-and-paper practice**.
    *   Focus heavily on **Graph Theory** and **Combinatorics** as they usually carry high weightage.

2.  **Operating Systems (OS):**
    *   Split your study into 50% Theory (Memory Management/Synchronization) and 50% Numericals (Scheduling/Page Replacement/Banker's Algorithm).
    *   Numericals are scoring—make sure you get full marks there.

3.  **Database Management Systems (DBMS):**
    *   Master **Normalization** and **Serializability**; these are guaranteed exam questions.
    *   Practice writing **SQL Queries** and **Relational Algebra** side-by-side.
